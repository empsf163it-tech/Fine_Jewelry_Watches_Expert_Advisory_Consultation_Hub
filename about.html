The Aurelis Charter

Built on uncompromising integrity.

Our advisory is strictly independent. We exist solely to protect your taste, your capital, and your long-term joy.

01

Absolute Independence

We hold no inventory and accept zero consignment kickbacks or dealer commissions. When we say "pass", there is no conflict of interest.

02

Originality First

We champion unpolished cases, honest patina, and period-correct components. We actively steer clients away from cosmetically over-polished compromises.

03

Strict Confidentiality

Private client registries, vault holdings, and acquisition amounts are protected under formal non-disclosure agreements at all times.

04

Holistic Wardrobe Fit

A great watch should flatter your hands, your skin tone, and your sartorial rhythm. We never advise in a mechanical vacuum.
