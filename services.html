Verification standards

Four pillars of mechanical truth.

Our verification protocol leaves no question unanswered before funds are transferred.

01. Calibre Health & Amplitude Timing machine testing, beat error, amplitude analysis, and service history validity.
02. Case Geometry & Chamfers Micro-inspection for over-polishing, original bevel lines, and crisp lug proportions.
03. Dial Typography & Lume Consistency Stereomicroscopy of fonts, serif weight, hand-aging, and UV reaction checks.
04. Archival Extracts & Provenance Direct manufacture ledger verification, original certificates, and stolen register clearance.
Jewelry & Gemset Protocol

Stones tested to laboratory standards.

Fine jewelry timepieces require gemological scrutiny equal to their mechanical movements. We ensure stones are factory-set, not aftermarket additions that harm residual value.

Factory vs. Custom Set Verification Verification against manufacture delivery specifications to guarantee factory origin.
Color & Clarity Harmony Matching pavilion angles, pavé symmetry, and uniform color grades across bezels.
Independent Laboratory Certification Coordination with GIA, Gübelin, and SSEF labs for high-carat colored stones and diamonds.
